## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: My PLC isn't showing up on the network scan.**
A: Make sure your device and the PLC are on the same local network, and that the app has local network access permission enabled in Settings.

**Q: How do I restore my Pro subscription on a new device?**
A: Open the app and use the "Restore Purchases" option, signed in with the same Apple ID / Google account used for the original purchase.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Subscriptions are managed through the App Store or Google Play, not within the app itself. Go to your device's subscription settings to manage or cancel.
